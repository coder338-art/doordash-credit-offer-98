# Target Credit Offer

Create a simple, centered, mobile-friendly promotional landing page for a $500 Target credit offer.

At the top, include a clean gift-card-style visual with no logo or brand name.

Below the visual, add the main headline:

“Get a $500 Target Credit”

Add a short, neutral supporting line:

“Complete the steps below to participate and receive your reward once verified.”

Then add a step-by-step list with exactly these 4 steps:

1. Click the button below

2. Enter your email & basic info

3. Complete 3 to 5 required deals

4. Receive your reward once verified

Place a large call-to-action button below the steps with the text:

“Get Started”

Under the button, add a small neutral note:

“Available in selected countries - US, UK, AU, CA”

Add a small disclosure near the bottom:

“This promotion is not affiliated with or endorsed by Target unless otherwise stated by the offer provider.”

Keep the design minimal, clean, modern, trustworthy, and mobile-friendly. Use generous spacing, clear typography, and a simple visual hierarchy.

Do not use words like “free,” “limited time,” “instant,” “guaranteed,” “100%,” or other urgency-based or misleading language.

Do not add extra steps, sections, navigation menus, testimonials, countdown timers, or a footer.
